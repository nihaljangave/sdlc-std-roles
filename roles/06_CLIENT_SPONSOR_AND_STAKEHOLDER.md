# Standard Role Playbook: Client Sponsor & Stakeholder

| Role Profile | Details |
|---|---|
| **Role Title** | Client Sponsor / Business Stakeholder |
| **Primary Accountability** | Business Objectives Alignment, Timely Scope Clarification & Formal Acceptance |
| **Key Collaborators** | Product Owner, Delivery Project Director, QA Lead |
| **Primary Gate Ownership**| Gate 0 (Charter Approval) & Gate 3 (UAT Acceptance Sign-Off) |

---

## 1. Core Mission & Objectives
The Client Sponsor ensures that the prototype or product aligns with commercial goals and enterprise strategy. By participating actively in scheduled agile ceremonies, providing feedback within agreed response times, and adhering to the formal change management process, they enable the engineering team to move at maximum velocity without risk of misaligned deliverables.

---

## 2. Responsibilities by SDLC Stage

### Stage 0: Discovery & Scoping
- Articulate the core business problem, user pain points, and strategic priorities.
- Review and approve the **Project Charter & Statement of Work (SOW)**.
- Define what constitutes an acceptable prototype / MVP (agreeing on Must-Have vs Won't-Have scope).
- Sign the Project Charter to pass **Gate 0**.

### Stage 1: Architecture & Design
- Designate client technical and compliance liaisons (e.g., enterprise architect, security officer).
- Facilitate delivery of client-side credentials, API sandbox access, and network whitelistings.
- Review and approve UI/UX wireframes within 48 hours of presentation.

### Stage 2: Development & Build
- Attend bi-weekly **Sprint Demos** to inspect working software.
- Provide consolidated feedback within 24 hours of each demo.
- Submit any new feature requests through the formal **Change Request Form** to avoid disrupting active sprints.

### Stage 3: Verification & Client UAT
- Appoint qualified client domain experts to execute the **Client UAT Scripts**.
- Conduct UAT testing within the scheduled testing window (typically 5 business days).
- Classify reported defects according to the agreed Severity Matrix (P1–P4).
- Execute the **Formal Client UAT Sign-Off Certificate** upon resolution of P1/P2 issues to clear **Gate 3**.

### Stage 4: Release & Ops Handover
- Approve the scheduled production release window.
- Ensure internal client teams (customer service, marketing, operations) are trained and ready for release.
- Participate in the Go/No-Go cutover decision.

### Stage 5: Post-Production & Hypercare
- Review weekly SLA and performance reports during the hypercare warranty period.
- Execute the final **Warranty Sign-Off** at the conclusion of hypercare to close the engagement.

---

## 3. Client Fast-Track Responsibilities & Service Level Agreements (SLAs)
To maintain prototype velocity without documentation delays:
- **Decision Turnaround SLA:** Business and architectural clarifications answered within **24 hours**.
- **Demo Feedback SLA:** Written demo feedback returned within **24 hours**.
- **UAT Completion SLA:** Test scenarios executed within **5 business days** of environment delivery.
- **Change Request Policy:** Scope modifications exceeding 5 story points undergo formal timeline/cost re-baselining.
